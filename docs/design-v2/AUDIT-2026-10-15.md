# Design v2 — app-wide audit (15.10.2026)

> Read-only audit of the end-user app (`src/app` non-admin, `src/features`, `src/components`; 610 `.tsx` files, admin excluded unless noted) against design language v2 (`.claude/knowledge/design-language.md`). Counts are grep counts at origin/main `72628338`.

## 1. What the app uses today

**Tokens.** Tailwind defines `primary #00dcd0`, `secondary #ea1d24`, `out-blue #007aff`, `out-cyan #00ADEF`, `on-brand #0F172A`, `card-light`/`background-light #f5f5f7`/`card-dark #1E293B`/`background-dark #0F172A`. There is one gradient, `brand-cta`. Shadows are `subtle | floating | drawer | premium | premium-hover | card`. Radii override Tailwind: `lg 10 | xl 12 | 2xl 14 | 3xl 20 | 4xl 28`. There are no Tailwind keyframes. Fonts: Simpler Pro 400/600/700, with 800/900 mapped to Bold.

Ad-hoc button patterns:

## 4. Recommendations beyond the screen PRs

1. **Icons: keep lucide.** 357 files use it; Phosphor isn't installed; a migration is churn without user value. Style lucide via `IconTile` (strokeWidth 2.4). Retire **Material Icons Round** (11 files plus an external font request at startup) in favour of lucide. That's a cheap cleanup.
2. **One shared activity/amenity icon map** (type → lucide icon) to replace the about 9 duplicated emoji maps.
3. **Collapse the 8 cyans** to `v2-accent`/`v2-cyan` and `v2-deep` for text. Fix the about 27 white-on-cyan lines as part of whichever screen PR touches them.
4. **Bundle Simpler Pro Black (900)** from the site. Today `font-black` (786 uses) renders as Bold.
5. **Dark mode:** decide whether `dark:` (1,496 classes) is live. Until then, v2 primitives are light-first, with explicit dark variants (`Card variant="dark"`).
