# Brand Voice — Routing Guide

**Source:** `brand-personal.md` §3 (personal profile) + `brand-company.md` §5 (app/company profile)
**Purpose:** Skills that need to apply a voice profile load this file first to identify which document contains the full profile rules.

---

## Profile: `personal` — קול דוד האישי

**Load:** `.claude/knowledge/brand-personal.md` — sections §3, §3.5, §4
**Use when:** Content for David's personal channels (@david.move26, LinkedIn personal, TikTok)

**Tone blend (per §3):** כן + אנרגטי + מעורר השראה + מומחה

**Quick rules:**
- First person, honest, human
- Shares dilemmas and failures — not just wins
- Connects everything to mission: health accessible to everyone, outdoors, together
- Ends with value or invitation — never a direct sell

**Guardrail check (§3.5 — mandatory):** Before finalizing any post for David's channels, apply the B2G filter:
"אם ראש מחלקת ספורט בעירייה יראה את זה — זה מחזק או מערער את האמון?" → If undermines trust: reframe as lesson, not complaint.

---

## Profile: `app` / `company` — קול OUT

**Load:** `.claude/knowledge/brand-company.md` — sections §2, §3, §5
**Use when:** Content for company channels (@outapp.il, presentations, emails to municipalities, landing pages, LinkedIn company)

**Tone blend (per §5):** חם + מעורר השראה + מקצועי ואמין

**Channel calibration:**
- Municipalities (email, presentation, LinkedIn): lean professional + credible, touch of inspiring
- End users (Instagram, app): lean warm + energetic + cool, with professional credibility in background

**Quick rules:**
- Never generic — always connects to the specific municipality's equipment or needs
- Anchors in the 4 messaging pillars (brand-company.md §3)
- Lead with specific phrases ("בחינם, בעברית, ביחד") not buzz words
- Use Proof Points (§6) when building credibility with municipalities

---

## Mixing profiles — NEVER

Never mix profiles in a single post.
When content suits both channels → distribute with different angles:
- Personal = "למה בניתי את זה"
- Company = "מה זה נותן לכם"

(Source: `brand-personal.md` §1.5)
